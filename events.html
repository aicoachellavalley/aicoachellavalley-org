The Series

Hands-on AI for the Coachella Valley.

Saturday Morning AI: Idea Labs is a monthly series from AI Coachella Valley and the CSUSB Entrepreneurship Resource Center — hands-on labs in AI, agents, and agentic training for founders, builders, and teams across the valley.

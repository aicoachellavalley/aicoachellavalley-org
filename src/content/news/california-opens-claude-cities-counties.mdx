On June 29, California made Claude available to all state agencies at a 50% discount — and extended the same pricing and training to every city and county, Riverside County included. For a Coachella Valley operator, the shift is that local government is now positioned to run AI at scale, and the tools that mediate how residents find services are moving from pilot to procurement. What's legible to those tools is what gets surfaced.
